# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
This repository is an existing Next.js 16 / React 19 gallery of isolated, copyable landing-page templates.

## Users and purpose
Visitors browse templates and copy their source and rebuild briefs. Each route is an independent fictional brand or presentation.

## Current task
Add an original six-chapter presentation introducing rural Japanese life. It must use Japanese fonts, a moving illustrated hero, an activity carousel, and no more than three pastel colour families. All imagery and animation must be code-created. No image or video generation. The user delegates creative decisions and explicitly waives questions and approvals.

## Constraints
Keep existing templates intact. Register the new presentation in the gallery. Keep its source isolated and copyable. Support keyboard navigation, touch, reduced motion and meaningful no-JavaScript content. Do not deploy or commit without a request.
