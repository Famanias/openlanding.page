03 / DIRECTOR’S NOTESON LOOKING CLOSER

A NOTE FROM THE DIRECTOR

Stay a little
longer.

Sometimes the most honest part of a story is the moment just before it begins.

INT. A QUIET ROOM — LATE AFTERNOON

The light moves across the wall. Someone is about to speak, but doesn’t.

That is where I like to begin. With room for a gesture, a place, or a pause to tell us something we couldn’t have planned.

Working across nonfiction, fiction and imagined commissions, I make pictures that feel lived in. Small crews. Open eyes. Space for the unexpected.
