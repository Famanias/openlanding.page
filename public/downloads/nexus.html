03 · How We Work

From first seed to full bloom.

Great work cannot be forced on an assembly line, but it thrives with rhythm. Follow our four-chapter cultivation cycle—or jump directly to any stage below.

1
Chapter 01 · Weeks 1–2

Listen — Soil & Roots

We talk with your founders, your longest-tenured customers, and your skeptics. We walk the shop floor, read support tickets, and gather the raw human truths that spreadsheets miss.

Deliverable: The Rootbook (Field Research & Positioning Thesis)
2
Chapter 02 · Weeks 3–5

Imagine — Sketch & Clay

Before touching a screen, our art directors and writers work on paper and unglazed clay—exploring metaphors, character sketches, editorial headlines, and tactile territory boards.

Deliverable: Three Distinct Creative Worlds & Editorial Manifesto
3
Chapter 03 · Weeks 6–10

Make — Hand & Craft

We illustrate, sculpt, write, and code every asset in-house. From bespoke typography systems and packaging dielines to choreographed interactive web experiences.

Deliverable: Complete Campaign Assets, Brand System & Flagship Site

Chapter 04 · Ongoing

Grow — Tend & Harvest

A launch is only the first season. We measure real human resonance—repeat orders, organic story shares, and qualified inbound demand—iterating alongside your team.

Deliverable: Seasonal Growth Experiments & Creative Stewardship
