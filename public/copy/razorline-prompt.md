# MASTER BUILD PROMPT: RAZORLINE — RL-01 Performance Footwear

## 1. Role & Architecture Objective
Act as a Principal Design Technologist and Senior Frontend Architect. Build a complete, responsive, interaction-rich performance athletics DTC storefront for **RAZORLINE** featuring the **RL-01 Performance Footwear** series.

**Core Concept:**
Uncompromising velocity. Engineered rebellion. Direct-to-consumer launch flagship for the RAZORLINE RL-01 performance running series, built around an exposed hollow-cavity midsole geometry, matrix monofilament upper, and aggressive kinetic typography.

---

## 2. Design System, Color Tokens & Typography

### Color Palette
- Carbon Black: `#0B0B0A` (canvas ground and dark container panels)
- Chalk Bone: `#F1F0E9` (high-contrast typography and structural hairline borders)
- Voltage Lime: `#D3FF35` (primary electric kinetic accent, selection, and primary CTAs)
- Cobalt Blue: `#375CFF` (secondary electric accent, tension saddle straps, track details)
- Raw Steel: `#777A7A` (telemetry readouts, specs, and secondary metadata)

### Typography
- **Display / Headlines:** 'Barlow Condensed' (600, 700, 800, 900 italic) with `-6deg` skew (`transform: skewX(-6deg)`).
- **Body / Interface:** 'Plus Jakarta Sans' (400, 500, 600, 700) for clean legible copy.
- **Technical / Telemetry:** 'JetBrains Mono' (400, 500, 600) with tabular numbers for weights, offsets, drop metrics, and coordinates.

### Geometry & Texture
- 40px blueprint grid backdrop (`.bg-blueprint-grid`) and subtle radial halftone matrix (`.bg-halftone-dots`).
- Parallelogram clipped buttons (`.clip-slant-cta`) and chamfered technical cards (`.clip-slant-card`).

## 3. Section-by-Section Functional Specifications

### SECTION 1: Sticky Navigation Header
- **Left:** Geometric angular RAZORLINE SVG icon + slanted condensed wordmark.
- **Center:** Quick anchor navigation (`FOOTWEAR`, `TECHNOLOGY`, `ATHLETES`, `JOURNAL`).
- **Right:** Shopping bag trigger with live count badge + 'SHOP NOW' slanted CTA button.

### SECTION 2: Hero Showcase
- **Headline:** Slanted display title "BREAK THE LIMIT. ENGINEERED TO OUTRUN EXPECTATIONS."
- **Interactive Model Switcher:** Switch between `RL-01 VOLTAGE`, `RL-01 COBALT`, and `RL-01 SHADOW` with live colorway updates and specification sync.
- **Telemetry HUD:** Real-time acceleration constants, latitude markers, energy return rates, and stack heights.
- **Actions:** 'EXPLORE THE COLLECTION' and 'DISCOVER THE TECHNOLOGY'.

### SECTION 3: Technical Marquee Ticker
- Continuous ticker showcasing cadence metrics, pace benchmarks, weight specifications (168g), and brand axioms.

### SECTION 4: Footwear Collection Grid
- Comprehensive product display featuring colorway cards, price tags, interactive US shoe size selector (7.5 to 13.0), instant Add to Bag, and Quick View trigger.

### SECTION 5: Exploded Breakaway Architecture
- Technical breakdown highlighting:
  1. Adaptive Sole Geometry (3-chamber mechanical void layout).
  2. Engineered Matrix Mesh (carbon-thread monofilament upper).
  3. Precise Heel Support (cantilevered Achilles cradle).

### SECTION 6: Technology Deep-Dive
- Tabbed interactive inspection chapters with coordinate telemetry, millimeter metrics, and hotspot inspection markers.

### SECTION 7: Field Testing Athletes
- Field test reports, session telemetry, and runner quotes from Berlin, Tokyo, and Zurich testing sectors.

### SECTION 8: Visual Lookbook
- Multi-frame studio and track photography viewer with carousel controls and technical aspect ratio labels.

### SECTION 9: Launch Reservation & Footer
- Drop notification signup form with email validation and error feedback.
- Architectural specs, site navigation, and copyright metadata.

### SECTION 10: Slide-Out Cart Drawer & Quick View Modal
- Cart drawer with line item list, size display, quantity increment/decrement, and subtotal calculation.
- Quick view modal with detailed model inspection, specs, and direct add-to-bag action.
