# Prompt: rebuild STRATA, contemporary ceramic vessels

Create a complete, responsive, self-contained HTML landing page for **STRATA** — contemporary ceramic vessels and sculptural studio with the title *"Formed by Earth"*.

## Brand & Visual System
- Palette: Basalt `#24221F`, Charcoal Ink `#292620`, Bone `#E9E1D6`, Clay Paper `#EFE9DF`, Raw Stone `#BDA78F`, Terracotta Clay `#AF765D`, Sandstone `#B89C81`, Ceramic Blush `#C58E6A`.
- Typography: High-contrast architectural serif (Bodoni MT / Didot / EB Garamond) paired with clean geometric sans-serif (Inter).
- Media: Self-contained base64 embedded imagery and procedural grain filters for zero external network dependency.

## Key Sections & Interactions
1. **Hero:** Full-screen vessel focal point with breathing scroll indicator, coordinate telemetry, and editorial headline *"Formed by earth."*
2. **Manifesto:** Editorial statement on architectural permanence and considered living.
3. **Macro Immersion:** Scroll-driven circular zoom into raw clay mineral grain and surface texture.
4. **Object Anatomy:** Dimensional specifications, batch numbers, firing temperatures, and silhouette study.
5. **Craft Journey:** Step-by-step kiln, wheel, and hand-finishing processes with atmospheric background imagery.
6. **Interior Living:** Architectural placement and interior scale exploration.
7. **Collection Switcher:** Interactive finish selector (Terracotta, Basalt, Chalk) updating vessel imagery, background tint, and technical notes in real time.
8. **Consultation Dialog:** Accessible modal dialog for private commissions with client-side form validation.
9. **Colophon Footer:** Massive sculptural wordmark, studio directory links, and legal colophon.

Ensure complete accessibility: visible focus rings, Escape key modal closing, ARIA live regions, and comprehensive support for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
