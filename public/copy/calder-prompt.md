# Prompt: rebuild “CALDER & ROWE” (editorial law-firm landing page)

Build a one-page landing site in Next.js App Router + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4 for CALDER & ROWE, an independent law firm for leaders navigating decisions with no easy precedent.

Use only black `#000000`, dark blue `#102B3F`, and white `#FFFFFF`. Use Cormorant Garamond for the legal display voice, Source Serif 4 for body copy, and Lato for navigation, metadata, forms, and interactive elements. Keep the tone established, practical and easy to scan. Avoid abstract tech motifs, accent colors, glass cards, oversized concept-page decoration and generic AI landing-page patterns.

Create a sticky topbar with a circular CR mark, the wordmark, legal-services navigation, a visible phone number and a “Schedule a consultation” CTA. The hero should state the firm’s specialty and location, then offer a primary consultation action and a secondary services link. Include a restrained architectural office panel as a supporting visual.

Use ordinary document scrolling with five sections:

1. Legal services — four concrete practice areas with short descriptions.
2. Our firm — an attorney profile, experience facts and a direct-attention promise.
3. Why clients call us — one testimonial.
4. FAQs — three expandable questions.
5. Contact — phone, email, office address and a local consultation form with a success state.

Reveal content with IntersectionObserver using opacity and translateY only. Keep all legal content in normal flow with no parallax rail or abstract 3D treatment. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, preserve visible keyboard focus, and include a legal disclaimer that the form does not create an attorney-client relationship.
