# Prompt: rebuild NOMI

Build a light-mode landing page in Next.js 16 (App Router), React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS v4 for fictional NOMI, a calm mobile workspace for independent tutoring studios. The one action is requesting a private walkthrough. Do not copy any real brand's marks, screenshots or trade dress.

**Look.** Warm paper with a faint fibre grain, tinted translucent "plastic" surfaces (a pale tint, a white glint along the top edge, a deeper shade at the foot, a soft tinted shadow beneath), green-black ink and one pharmacy green for every signal. Palette in OKLCH: paper `oklch(97.6% 0.011 88)`, paper-2 `oklch(96.5% 0.02 160)`, paper-3 `oklch(93% 0.036 160)`, ink `oklch(21% 0.025 170)`, accent `oklch(50% 0.13 160)`, and one tint per moment: inbox `oklch(87% 0.048 210)`, schedule `oklch(91.5% 0.064 98)`, progress `oklch(86.5% 0.075 160)`, payments `oklch(84% 0.048 322)`. Fonts through `next/font/google`: Schibsted Grotesk 700 for display, Instrument Sans 400 and 500 for text, DM Mono only for times and figures. Every colour and font is a token on `:root`.

**Page.** Slab header (full-width solid bar: wordmark, Product, Studios, Plans, a "Book a demo" action; it hides on scroll-down, returns on scroll-up, a sliding underline marks the section in view, a hairline progress rule runs along its foot). Then:
1. Hero: "Make every lesson feel looked after." with one sentence and "Request a walkthrough" plus "Explore the app". Beside it, a stack of four plastic cards (Inbox, Schedule, Progress, Payments) that rise into place one after another, with a "Settled" stamp landing last. The stack lifts away as the page scrolls on.
2. Launch: "One surface. Four calm moments." A pinned stage (a section about 4.5 screens tall with a `position: sticky` child). On the left a numbered rail of the four moments; on the right one phone on a tinted plastic band. Scroll progress picks the moment; choosing a moment from the rail scrolls to it. The active rail row gets a sliding pill, a line draws from it across to the band, the band and the phone's shell fade to that moment's tint, and the screen swaps with a small directional slide (Inbox threads, Schedule day strip and slots, Progress ring and skill bars that fill, Payments ledger). A bottom sheet carries the headline "You have 3 new threads", "Your week, with room", "Progress worth sharing" or "Everything settled". Keep `.nm-phone` and a single `.nm-app-card strong`. Unpinned, with a tab row, under 60rem and under reduced motion.
3. Studios: "Quiet tools for busy humans." Three full-width rows split by hairlines (Know what is next / Show the progress / Close the loop), each with its line and a small built sample panel. Rows rise in a stagger and the hairlines draw.
4. Plans: "Less admin. More attention." and one offer ticket, NOMI Studio at $39 per month for up to 4 tutors, three included items whose check marks draw on, a perforated stub holding "Start a conversation".
5. Demo: "Give your studio a softer day." with a form on a sunken tray: name, studio email, a students select (1–25, 26–75, 76+), "Request a walkthrough". A tilted paper tag above the tray fills in live as the visitor types. An invalid email shakes the field and shows the message; success cross-fades to a drawn check and "We’ll make room." The form posts nowhere (`data-endpoint=""`) and says so on screen.
6. Footer: a giant wordmark over the links and the legal line.

**Motion.** Only `transform`, `opacity`, `clip-path` and stroke offsets move. Load: header drops, headline words rise through masks, sentence and actions rise, cards arrive. Scroll: masks for headings, rise for text, a clip-path wipe for the offer and the tray (clip the child, never the observed element), staggered rows with drawn rules. Hover is one signal per control: a fill wipe on buttons, a lift on the hero cards; every control sinks 3px on press. Hidden pre-reveal states live under a `.js` class and `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, so no-script and reduced-motion visitors get the finished page. No loops.

**Quality bar.** One `h1`, 16px body minimum, visible focus rings, 44 to 52px controls, mobile layout at 375px with no sideways scroll, no external media, one icon style (inline SVG), isolated to `src/app/(nomi)/`.
