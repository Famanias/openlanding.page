# STORYLINES

## Chosen — Gallery as a door

**Device:** one cursor carries the viewer through the gallery. **Form:** object-to-object chain with click-led iris transitions.

The film begins inside the real openlandingpage gallery, where the cursor finds a field of previews. Each click enlarges one card until its template becomes the full frame; its most distinctive visual detail holds, then the cursor exits toward the next card. The viewer feels the variety as a single curated space rather than a list of screenshots.

**First two seconds:** the real gallery screenshot is already on screen; the cursor glides from the header toward the first chosen card and lands on WOWFLUTTER.

**Beats:** gallery display → WOWFLUTTER → NOCTILUCA → KEYTU → TRUECUT Works → TRACE → VOLUME ZERO → LOOPHOUSE → IRIDIA → BATHYS → open landing worlds.

**Look:** source gallery dark graphite field, warm white type, acid-lime focus mark, real preview images, thin hairlines, no invented UI chrome.

**Sound:** cinematic non-lyrical instrumental, restrained electronic pulse with a slow build; one soft click at each card selection and a longer low whoosh on each world change.

## Alternate — Three shelves

A triptych of nine shelves: editorial, spatial, and technical. It is calmer and more graphic, but the fixed grouping makes the click-through feel less like browsing.

## Alternate — Endless zoom

Each card becomes the next card's image in a chain of match cuts. It is the most abstract option, but it hides the gallery's real browsing behavior and risks making the templates feel like a mood reel rather than a usable library.
