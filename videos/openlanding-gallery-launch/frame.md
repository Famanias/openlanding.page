# Frame direction

- Canvas: 1920×1080 landscape.
- Palette: graphite `#0D0F0E`, warm white `#F2F0E8`, acid lime `#D8F7A2`, muted rule `#343936`.
- Typography: Inter for display and body; Space Mono for labels.
- Composition: real screenshot and preview plates are the source of truth. Use a full-bleed ground clip, then one focal image at a time. Keep titles inside the central 80% safe area.
- Motion: cursor-led click, iris from the click point, then one controlled push-in or parallax crop on the real preview. Land title copy before the image continues moving. Avoid UI reconstruction and avoid decoration that is not present in the source.
- Audio: no narration; cinematic non-lyrical instrumental bed. Clicks and whooshes are optional accents only when the local sound files are available.
