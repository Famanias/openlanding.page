## Intent

Feature the site's own captured gallery and real template preview assets. The film behaves like a cursor-led tour: start with the gallery display, then click through the selected templates in the supplied order. Each template gets an individual visual reveal of its most distinctive area, without pretending to show every section of the page.

## Customizations

- No voiceover.
- Cinematic non-lyrical background music.
- 1920×1080 landscape output.
- Use the real template card previews and named detail plates from the repository.
- Keep copy sparse and sourced from the gallery/template names; no invented performance claims.
