## Video direction

The film is a cursor-led tour of the real gallery. Preserve the source gallery's graphite ground and warm white type, use acid-lime only for focus/click states, and keep all template imagery at a readable scale. Each frame gets one hero move: click, zoom, mask, or measured pan. Titles land and hold. Transition causes alternate between iris from click, zoom through the selected card, cut on click, and blur swap so the piece feels hand-directed rather than templated.
