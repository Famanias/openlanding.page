# MORROW — repair, kept local

## Concept

Morrow is a neighborhood repair network. The site tells one physical route from a broken object to a nearby workbench, then explains the two service modes, local makers, FAQs, and the booking CTA.

## Palette and type

- Paper `#F5EBDD`: warm background
- Ink `#1C2A26`: deep green-black text
- Brick `#B65344`: route markers and primary accent
- Ochre `#DFA75B`: illustration and secondary emphasis
- DM Serif Display for display type, Work Sans for body copy, IBM Plex Mono for labels.

## Structure

1. Hero: “The fix starts close to home.” with a neighborhood route illustration.
2. How a repair travels: four numbered stops.
3. Two ways to keep it going: service cards plus distance calculator.
4. The workbench map: local makers.
5. People on the route: quote and network metrics.
6. FAQs and final repair intake CTA.

## Motion

Content reveals through opacity and translateY only. The calculator is interactive and local. Reduced motion removes reveal transitions.
