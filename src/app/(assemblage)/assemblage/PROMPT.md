# Rebuild Assemblage

Build the Assemblage landing page in Next.js 16 App Router, React 19 and TypeScript. It is a mixed media material study for independent brands. The primary action is **Request a material study**.

Use Manrope with Geist Mono for utility labels. Use only these palette colours: paper `#F6F0E8`, ink `#1E1D1B`, sand `#E2D8C9`, vermilion `#D95F3F`, teal `#1D6C63`, ochre `#E7C65A`, plum `#8F4E70`. Use flat backgrounds, layered collage shapes, paper shadows, tape strips, registration marks and no stock imagery.

Sections are a floating header, collage hero, four benefits, two line word by word tagline reveal, three step method, interactive material index, proof cards, six question FAQ, local request form with validation and success state, and footer. The material index is the signature: selecting Paper, Ink, Image or Signal raises that layer and changes the detail copy. Use IntersectionObserver for one time reveals, complete keyboard focus states, pressed button feedback and reduced motion fallbacks. Keep all content visible with JavaScript disabled. Keep imports inside `src/app/(assemblage)/`.

The collage must be an interactive Three.js paper studio with curled geometry, printed canvas textures, directional shadows, translucent acetate, raycast dragging, release momentum and spring settling. Provide Assemble, Unpack, Recompose, material selection and keyboard repositioning. Reuse the engine in the material index with spread and lighting sliders. On tall desktops pin the hero for a dimensional departure and give the method a sticky visual that assembles scraps across three reversible chapters. Use native scroll, cap rendering resolution and pause settled or offscreen scenes. Preserve authored HTML artwork when WebGL or JavaScript is unavailable; reduced motion keeps the controls functional with immediate updates. Retain native touch scrolling. The dependency is `three`.

Render the paper full bleed on a viewport-wide transparent canvas with vertical overscan. Preserve its scale with a camera view offset. Sheets and shadows must extend outside their scene div without rectangular clipping or a background slab. Keep the controls aligned below and pointer events passing through empty canvas space.
