# DAYMARK

## Requirements and concept
An architectural study of sunlight: the sun traces a roof, so visitors can explore an illustrative day before considering a solar plan. All data and inquiry actions are demonstrations.

## Palette and type
Limestone #F0E8D9, cobalt #2C4EEA, copper #C46D48, graphite #252821 and sunflower #F6D873. System grotesk and monospace keep the page fast and architectural.

## Layout and mechanic
A diagonal house image and typographic rail lead into a sticky daylight study. Time maps from 06:00 to 18:00, using x = 10 + 80t and y = 70 - 52e sin(pi t). Seasonal elevation and roof notes are qualitative, not a calculation of solar production. Survey, shape and review lead into editable project factors and a local inquiry.

## Motion
Personality: measured and optimistic. Verbs: trace and reveal. Signature: the sun travels an arc over a fixed home. Hero image wipes in, headline rises, the line draws and controls respond to state. Reduced motion keeps all content visible.

## Signature details / do and don't
Coordinate labels, copper arcs, ruled sections. Keep readings explicitly illustrative. Never invent production, savings, bookings or backend submission.
