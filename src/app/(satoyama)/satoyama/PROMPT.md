# Prompt: rebuild "Satoyama Biyori" — a slower kind of day

Build an original illustrated rural-Japan presentation in Next.js 16 App Router, React 19 and TypeScript. Brand: 里山日和 / Satoyama Biyori. It is a six-page picture book, with persistent chapter navigation, page dots, a counter, and previous/next arrows. It is a cultural introduction, not a tourism booking site.

The central idea: a quiet day is a picture book, so navigation turns pages through rural landscapes, daily rituals, small adventures and the seasons.

Use three pastel families: cream #F6F3E8, sage #ADC3A1 and dusty rose #D7AAA0, plus dark forest ink #2F4438 for readable text. Three scene-only accents serve the painted skies and autumn: pastel sky blue #9CC4D6, warm sun #F1D9A2 and ember #C8714F. Derive every other landscape and UI tone with color-mix. Fonts through next/font/google: Shippori Mincho 400 for display and all Japanese text, DM Sans for English navigation/body. Disable CJK preloading. Use real scoped CSS custom properties for fonts and motion tokens, so webpack and Turbopack render identically without a Tailwind processing dependency.

The six chapters: Welcome; The landscape; A slower rhythm; Little adventures; The seasons; Until next time. Hero title: “A little closer to nature. A little closer to yourself.” Use oversized literary typography over an open sky, a dark leaf-motif action button, a vertical Japanese poem, and an immersive summer meadow below. Surround the scene with a cream folio frame. Header has an original mountain mark and Japanese lockup, all six chapter links and “Take the slow way.” Bottom has bilingual page labels, six dots, and arrow controls.

All artwork MUST be fully coded original SVG, never generated imagery, stock imagery or video. Paint it as 2D anime backgrounds in the spirit of Hayao Miyazaki's hand-painted scenery: gradient skies, towering cumulus with lavender undersides, layered hazy ridges, clumped leafy canopies lit from the upper left, wind-leaning grass and warm lit windows. Every slide has its own scenery, never a reused picture: Welcome is a summer meadow with a great tree on a hill; The landscape is terraced paddies mirroring the sky under misty cedar ridges; A slower rhythm is an afternoon village beside a stone canal with laundry out; Little adventures is a pale bamboo grove with sunbeams; Until next time is a dusk with the sun sinking behind ridges, paddies holding gold light, paper lanterns and fireflies. The seasons are four separate compositions, not recolours: cherry blossom over flooded paddies, summer rows of rice beneath great clouds and sunflowers, autumn paddies with rice drying on racks and a red maple, and a snowbound village with a glowing window. Share primitives (cumulus, ridge, painted tree, farmhouse, blades of grass) between scenes. Animate restrained groups: drifting clouds, mist, chimney smoke, swaying grass and bamboo, birds, falling petals, leaves or snow, fireflies. Provide a pause button, stop animation in hidden tabs and inactive pages, and stop all animation under reduced motion. Use soft gradients rather than outlines and no external assets. Do not copy existing film scenes or characters.

The landscape chapter explains satoyama as the meeting place of village and mountain. Daily rhythm is a three-entry illustrated journal, with morning, afternoon and evening. Adventures has five illustrated activity postcards: rice fields, tea, cycling, gardening and river walks. Build a looping carousel with three cards on desktop, one on mobile, next/previous controls, a live counter, swipe gestures and keyboard support. Seasons has four accessible tabs that crossfade between four different seasonal scenes and change the caption. The final chapter has a rose dusk, reflective copy and a restart button.

Keep navigation logic pure and tested: clamp book edges, resolve valid hashes and wrap activity indices. Update hash on navigation; support browser back and forward and direct links. Arrow keys/Home/End navigate the book outside buttons, links and inputs. Preserve native Space and Enter. Inactive hydrated pages are inert. Without JavaScript show the complete book in normal flow. Use visible focus rings, meaningful SVG descriptions, no horizontal overflow at 375px and large touch controls.

Motion verbs: drift and turn. Use a layered directional page transition, masked heading entrances, short body rises, picture hover lift and button fill wipes. Tokens: 90/180/420/800/1200ms, ease-out cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1), turn cubic-bezier(.77,0,.175,1). Animate only transform and opacity continuously. Organise into brand, illustrations, chapters, deck and motion folders. Keep template imports isolated. Register the route at all six gallery points, capture a real browser preview, add e2e coverage, and run repository checks, unit tests and TypeScript. Do not deploy or commit.
