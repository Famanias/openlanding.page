# Prompt: rebuild "Studio Kazeko"

Build an isolated `/kazeko` landing page in Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS v4 for a fictional independent Japanese-inspired animation studio. Its motto is "A little wonder. A world of feeling." The central idea: every film begins with a pencil mark, so scrolling turns an original countryside sketch into a coloured, moving scene.

## Brand
Palette: paper #F8F5E9, ink #243C35, meadow #64856A, leaf #C7D6A7, sky #D6E7EB, butter #EED998, clay #B96C51, muted ink #596C63. Use color-mix for tints. Display: Shippori Mincho 400, body: DM Sans 400/500, labels: IBM Plex Mono 400. Load via next/font/google; preload false for Japanese fonts. Original inline SVG bird-and-breeze mark, small STUDIO over the literary kazeko. wordmark. Mood: gentle, nostalgic, airy, painterly, curious. Soft 6px artwork corners, pill actions, editorial layouts, abundant negative space.

## Sections
1. Sticky cream header: logo, Our films / Our craft / The studio, and Let's make something. Phone menu is a native modal dialog with focus containment, Escape close and scroll locking.
2. Full-width pale-blue sky hero: small independent-animation eyebrow, centred large serif two-line headline, "We draw the little things. And the worlds they become.", Step into our stories CTA. Bottom 72% is an original panoramic SVG landscape: layered rolling hills, little clay-roofed house, pale sun, paths, field hatching, wildflowers, clouds and paper bird. Paper silhouette overlaps the lower edge; three small captions finish the scene.
3. Selected films in an asymmetric two-column grid: large portrait The Last Light, smaller wide Where the River Goes and A Pocketful of Spring. Each has an original illustrated placeholder, year, type, Japanese subtitle and accessible details dialog. Clearly describe these as fictional film concepts; do not pretend there is playable film footage.
4. First, a line. Then, a little life. A 190vh sticky process scene has geometrically matching pencil and colour SVG layers. Progress = clamp((scrollY-start)/(sectionHeight-viewportHeight),0,1). Colour layer clip-path inset right = (1-progress)*100%. A labelled native range, Pencil / Colour / Life buttons and Resume scroll control support mouse/keyboard/touch. Explicit manual control persists until Resume. Phone and reduced-motion layouts unpin the stage.
5. A small studio. A very open window. Portrait illustration of a countryside drawing desk and a thoughtful essay about noticing ordinary details; services are original shorts, brand animation, music videos and imagined worlds.
6. Dark-green contact section: Something on your mind? Client-only form with name, email and idea. Validate trimmed required values, bounded lengths and sensible email format. Explain errors with aria-invalid and describedby. Focus first invalid field. Loading state becomes an accessible success panel, focused on arrival, with a folded bird and a downloadable text brief. Explicitly state nothing was sent or stored. Escape user text through React; never interpolate it into HTML.
7. Cream footer with logo, motto, back-to-top link and fictional-studio disclosure. Global accessible pause/resume control for ambient movement.

## Motion
Personality organic/analog; verbs drift and unfurl. One signature scene transforms pencil into colour. CSS-first load sequence: sky settles, header drops at 150ms, headline line masks at 250/350ms, body and CTA rise at 550/650ms. Use mask, rise, wipe, scale and drawn-line scroll reveals. Shared IntersectionObserver reveals once. Ambient clouds, grass and paper bird are at most three groups and pause offscreen, when hidden, and under user pause/reduced motion. Pure tested progress math; requestAnimationFrame throttled passive scroll listeners; no per-frame React state. Lenis is dynamically loaded only for fine desktop pointers without reduced motion. All observers/listeners/RAF/engines are cleaned up.

Tokens: strong ease-out cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1), expo cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), handoff cubic-bezier(.77,0,.175,1), gentle spring; durations 90/180/420/800/1200ms; 24px shift, 70ms stagger. Hero stays visible without hydration. Reveal hiding only activates after JS setup; all content is visible without JS and under reduced motion. Gate hover transforms to fine pointers. Cards lift and illustrations zoom; buttons wipe and press to .97; fine-pointer CTAs have <=8px magnetism. Preserve visible keyboard rings and reserve all art boxes.

## Assets, structure and verification
Keep brand, sections, motion, process, forms and illustration modules inside the route group. page.tsx and content sections are server components; interactive leaves are client components. Include favicon, 1200×630 OG image, DESIGN.md, tested mechanic/validation, and e2e tests. Picture chooses generated 800/1600w AVIF/WebP via a manifest and otherwise shows labelled SVG concept art. Artwork is user-generated from docs/image-prompts/kazeko.md; raw assets go in assets/incoming/kazeko/. The hero and process scene remain original SVG for exact animation alignment. No API calls, stock assets or generation spend.

Register the palette, gallery metadata/prompt/preview, source-bundle dependencies (lenis only), image allowlist and shared e2e path. Test desktop and 390px touch, scroll controls, range keyboard operation, dialog focus and Escape, local form errors/success/download, navigation, reduced motion, no JS, overflow and page errors. Screenshot at 1440×900 and 390×844 plus 25/50/75% scroll. Check isolation, palette, unit tests, types and browser tests. Do not commit or deploy.
