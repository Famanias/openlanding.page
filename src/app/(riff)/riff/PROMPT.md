# Prompt: rebuild "Riff" (Music-First Dating & Zine Landing Page)

Build a complete, production-grade, single-page interactive music-first dating application landing page named **Riff** (*Fall for the sound before the selfie. A music-first 18+ dating app that matches people through playlists, genres, favorite artists, and listening chemistry.*).

## Brand & Central Idea

Riff rejects surface-level selfie swiping and visual ego games in favor of shared music culture: 18+ singles match through their record collections, favorite basement venues, zero-skip tracks, and nocturnal listening rituals. The visual language blends raw independent underground music zines with clean, modern mobile product UI: deep matte black `#0B0B0B` canvas, tactile analog grain, paper collage banners `#F4F0E8`, screenprint crimson `#C92533` accents, perforated concert ticket stubs, and spinning 12-inch vinyl records.

## Visual Design & Typography

- **Colors:** Canvas `#0B0B0B`, Elevated Panel `#141414`, Newsprint White `#F4F0E8`, Screenprint Crimson `#C92533`, Deep Red `#741B24`, Muted Grey `#AAA8A6`.
- **Typography:**
  - Headlines: Bodoni Moda (italic serif for editorial emotional pull).
  - Posters/Gig Badges: Barlow Condensed (bold, all-caps, concert poster feel).
  - Body / UI: Plus Jakarta Sans (clean, readable app UI).
  - Telemetry / Codes: JetBrains Mono (monospaced timestamps, track lengths, serial codes).
- **Textures:** Fixed pointer-events-none noise overlay, radial halftone screen patches, torn zine paper clip paths, and perforated concert ticket edges.

## Key Sections & Interactive Features

1. **Sticky Header:** Custom SVG waveform logo, navigation links (`How It Works`, `Matches`, `Playlists`, `Stories`), mobile hamburger menu, and screenprint red "Join Riff" button.
2. **Hero Section (Two Phones, One Chemistry):**
   - Dual-phone mockup showcasing real profile cards (Mila, 24 and Julian, 26).
   - Interactive vinyl record with realistic micro-grooves, specular sheen, and side A/B flipping.
   - Switchable shared tracks (`Kyoto`, `Starburster`, `Chaise Longue`) that update match badges and icebreakers in real time.
3. **Manifesto Section:**
   - Wheat-pasted torn paper banners: *"THERE'S MORE TO A PERSON THAN A PHOTO"*.
   - Expandable liner notes accordion exploring Riff's core thesis.
   - Three interactive product principles with expand/collapse details.
4. **How It Works (Three Tracks):**
   - Interactive tape-head progress rail scrubbable across Track 01 (Drop your favorites), Track 02 (Find your overlap), and Track 03 (Start a conversation).
   - Rotating perforated backstage paper ticket stub updating dynamically with station stamps.
5. **Interactive Taste Matcher:**
   - Left panel: clickable underground genres (Shoegaze, Post-Punk, Midwest Emo, Darkwave, etc.) and listening rituals (Night drives, Live gigs, Vinyl digging, Late-night headphones).
   - Right panel: real-time deterministic Jaccard + ritual overlap calculation against partner profiles, generating custom conversation starters and shared scene tags.
6. **Music Chemistry Case Study:**
   - Segmented control toggling between Favorite Records, Concert Energy, and Late-Night Mixes.
   - Dual-phone screen comparison connected by an unfurling torn paper bridge.
7. **The Scene Playlist Wall:**
   - Six mood-curated playlists with custom art sleeves and rotational offsets.
   - Interactive gatefold sleeve drawer opening tracklists, lengths, and copyable icebreaker prompts.
8. **Stories & Backstage Pass:**
   - Carousel of real connection stories from East London, Glasgow, and Chicago.
   - Client-side Backstage Pass stamper with input validation issuing a unique `#RF-XXXXXX` preview ticket.
   - Global motion toggle button for accessible reduced motion.
