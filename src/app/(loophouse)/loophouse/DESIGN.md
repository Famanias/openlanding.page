# LOOPHOUSE — tools, kept in reach

## Concept

LoopHouse is a neighborhood tool library. The site tells one physical route from a broken object to a nearby workbench, then explains the two service modes, local hosts, FAQs, and the booking CTA.

## Palette and type

- Paper `#EFF3E4`: warm background
- Ink `#17272A`: deep green-black text
- Brick `#2F6F67`: route markers and primary accent
- Ochre `#E3A52A`: illustration and secondary emphasis
- DM Serif Display for display type, Work Sans for body copy, IBM Plex Mono for labels.

## Structure

1. Hero: “The fix starts close to home.” with a neighborhood route illustration.
2. How a borrow travels: four numbered stops.
3. Two ways to keep it going: service cards plus distance calculator.
4. The workbench map: local hosts.
5. People on the route: quote and network metrics.
6. FAQs and final borrow intake CTA.

## Motion

Content reveals through opacity and translateY only. The calculator is interactive and local. Reduced motion removes reveal transitions.
