# Prompt: rebuild Pitchlane, AI-powered presentation platform

Create a complete, responsive, self-contained HTML landing page for **Pitchlane** — an AI-powered presentation platform with the tagline *"Make ideas land. From the first idea to the final slide."*

## Brand & Visual System
- Palette: Ink Indigo `#17182B`, Frost `#F7F8FF`, Brand Purple `#7654DF`, Soft Lilac `#E6DFFB`, Violet Accent `#7A66E9`, Coral Signal `#FF716C`, Sky Blue `#61BBFA`.
- Typography: Clean neo-grotesque sans-serif (Inter / PitchInter) across display, body, and UI elements.
- Embedded media: Inline base64 fonts and cover images to guarantee standalone offline usability.

## Key Sections & Interactions
1. **Hero:** High-impact value proposition *"Present ideas that actually land"*, call-to-action buttons, and an interactive slide preview deck.
2. **Slide Deck Showcase:** Interactive carousel of modern presentation templates (Architectural Geometry, Iridescent Forms, Clear Structure, Momentum, Fresh Perspective, Design Studio).
3. **Workflow & Features:** Intelligent slide layout, kinetic narrative structuring, real-time styling, and team collaboration tooling.
4. **Interactive Studio Demonstration:** Tabbed interface demonstrating prompt-to-deck transformation, slide editing, and export workflows.
5. **Social Proof & Testimonials:** Customer stories and quantitative performance metrics.
6. **Pricing & Sign-up:** Transparent tier comparison and newsletter/trial signup form with client-side validation.
7. **Footer:** Comprehensive site navigation links, social handles, and copyright information.

Keep all navigation, carousel buttons, modal dialogs, and form controls fully interactive and accessible via keyboard. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and ensure CLS is 0.
