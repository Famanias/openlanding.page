# MASTER BUILD PROMPT — STAR RIOT
## One-shot, motion-rich disco / nightlife landing page

> **For the implementation agent:** Build a brand-new, complete, polished, working landing page for **STAR RIOT**, a fictional disco and after-dark dance club. Deliver a high-voltage, interaction-rich Next.js experience.

---

## 01 — North-star creative direction

- **Brand:** STAR RIOT
- **Category:** Fictional neon disco club, dance floor, DJ nights, community, after-dark culture
- **Personality:** Unapologetic, expressive, cheeky, kinetic, colorful, inclusive, a little surreal, designed with craft
- **Positioning:** A neon-charged space where glitter, loud bass, good friends, and improbable nights collide
- **Primary headline:** `TONIGHT, WE GO SUPERNOVA.`
- **Primary CTA:** `GET ON THE LIST ↗`
- **Secondary CTA:** `EXPLORE THE NIGHTS`
- **Supporting copy:** `Disco fever. Future sounds. Zero ordinary nights. Step into the place where the dance floor becomes the whole universe.`

Think **retro pop-art disco meets contemporary bold creative studio**. Maintain a nearly black plum background, large tightly-set heavy white display type, huge electric starburst shapes, saturated mint-to-lime gradients, bubblegum pink accents, playful vector figures, angular motion motifs, small floating UI-window cards, sparkles, and a clear cinematic contrast.

## 03 — Typography and editorial system

- Headlines: oversized, aggressively bold, compact/condensed, UPPERCASE; tight leading (`Anton`, `Impact`, `Arial Narrow Bold`).
- Navigation and body: precise modern sans-serif (`Public Sans`), bold for UI labels, readable for descriptions.
- Microcopy: uppercase labels with generous tracking and playful separators such as `✦`, `↗`, `//`, `001`, or tiny star glyphs.

## 04 — Essential sections

1. **Header & Brand Navigation:** Custom split neon star glyph, primary links (`NIGHTS`, `THE SPACE`, `THE SOUND`, `FAQ`), mobile drawer.
2. **Hero Section (The Ignition):** Giant display type `TONIGHT, WE GO SUPERNOVA.`, live DJ lineup deck with BPM selector, interactive equalizer bars with transport controls, tonight set slots, colossal neon star portal, and vector disco dancers.
3. **Manifesto Section:** `ORDINARY NIGHTS? NOT HERE.`, 3 core club pillars, spinning vinyl record, and animated ticker marquee.
4. **Nights Section (Pick Your Frequency):** Frequency filter tabs (`All`, `House & Edits`, `Classic Disco`, `Soul & Boogie`), 3 poster cards with 3D pointer tilt, accessible event detail dialog modal with lineup breakdown.
5. **Sound Stage Section (The Sound Takes Over):** Scroll-controlled 3-stage progression (`01. WARM UP` → `02. TURN UP` → `03. ALL OUT`) with expanding bass rings, erupting supernova star, and live telemetry readout.
6. **Space Section (The Space):** 4-zone architectural collage featuring sunken sprung floor with real-time under-floor lighting matrix switcher, triple mirror-ball orbit, 360° floor-level booth, and hospitality crew.
7. **Movement Section (Voices From The 3 AM Floor):** Sticker photo-wall snap carousel with interactive peel note flips, plus FAQ accordion.
8. **Guestlist Conversion Section:** Priority floor access form with real-time validation, deterministic demo pass code generation (`SR-XXXX-YYY`), barcode graphic, and live RSVP counter.
9. **Site Footer:** Brand sign-off, quick links, fictional concept disclaimer, back-to-top control.
