# AFTERWALK — a little more outside

Build an isolated Next.js 16 / React 19 / TypeScript landing page for a fictional neighborhood walking club. Use Motion for React (`motion/react`) and no 3D. The central idea is that a good walk is a line between small discoveries: use original two-dimensional route maps and photographs as the visual thread, with a filterable walk finder and a local walk-card preview.

Use ink #1E3529, paper #F7F5EB, white #FBFAF3, lime #DAE870, sage #ABC6A2, peach #E9BA9F, butter #F3E7B6 and muted green #607168. Set Fraunces for display, DM Sans for reading and IBM Plex Mono for field notes. Create an original sunburst mark and a warm editorial art direction with five original, locally served walking photographs.

Build a split hero headed “Good things happen on foot.” with a photo, large typography and the primary “Find your kind of walk” anchor. Follow it with a plain green band, a draggable five-photo reel, a generous introduction, three sample routes with photographs and illustrated map details, filters for all walks, an hour or less, and after work, a photographic company section, a local walk-card form, a native FAQ and an oversized closing invitation. Selecting a route must preselect it in the form. The form validates a name and creates a local preview; it never claims to send a message or book a real meetup. Clearly label the club and routes as fictional.

Use expressive 2D motion: on wide, tall screens the hero pins and recedes as the next sheet and plain green band cover it; the photo reel loops continuously in one direction, accepts drag gestures, and offers pause and previous/next buttons; photographs parallax gently, filters reflow their cards and the local receipt transitions into view. Use mask and wipe reveals, tactile hover/press feedback, and a responsive mobile menu. Pause ambient motion offscreen or in hidden tabs and provide a still, native-scroll reel when reduced motion is requested or JavaScript is absent. Use semantic controls, visible focus, responsive images, accessible labels, no horizontal overflow and no cross-template imports. Include an icon, Open Graph image, design notes, a gallery preview, and tests for route filtering, scroll/drag, validation and the principal browser flow.
