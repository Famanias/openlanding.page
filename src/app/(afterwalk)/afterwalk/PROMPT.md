# AFTERWALK — a little more outside

Build an isolated Next.js 16 / React 19 / TypeScript landing page for a fictional neighborhood walking club. Use no 3D. The central idea is that a good walk is a line between small discoveries: use original two-dimensional route maps as the visual thread, with a filterable walk finder and a local walk-card preview.

Use ink #1E3529, paper #F7F5EB, white #FBFAF3, lime #DAE870, sage #ABC6A2, peach #E9BA9F, butter #F3E7B6 and muted green #607168. Set Fraunces for display, DM Sans for reading and IBM Plex Mono for field notes. Create an original sunburst mark and a warm editorial art direction with two original, locally served walking photographs.

Build a split hero headed “Good things happen on foot.” with a photo, large typography and the primary “Find your kind of walk” anchor. Follow it with a dark typographic band, a generous introduction, three illustrated sample routes, filters for all walks, an hour or less, and after work, a local walk-card form, a photographic company section, a native FAQ and an oversized closing invitation. Selecting a route must preselect it in the form. The form validates a name and creates a local preview; it never claims to send a message or book a real meetup. Clearly label the club and routes as fictional.

Use subtle 2D motion: rise, mask and photo wipe reveals, tactile hover and press feedback, and a responsive mobile menu. Respect reduced motion and retain meaningful content without JavaScript. Use semantic controls, visible focus, responsive images, accessible labels, no horizontal overflow and no cross-template imports. Include an icon, Open Graph image, design notes, a gallery preview, and tests for route filtering, validation and the principal browser flow.
