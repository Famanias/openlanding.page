# Prompt: rebuild “Avel” — more room for good thinking

Create an isolated, responsive landing page for Avel, a fictional AI company with several models. Use a wide, original coastal hero photograph with soft pastel brown sky, quiet limestone cliffs and deep pine water. Layer a polished AI product window into the hero, following the editorial feel of the supplied reference without copying a real product. Use a companion coastal photograph across the closing signup and footer, with light and dark treatments that keep text readable. Make “Get started” the primary CTA.

Use pastel clay #CDB3A3, ivory #F8F5EF, deep pine #243A32, white #FFFFFF, sand #E8DDD2 and muted pine #5A6A60. Pair Newsreader serif headlines with Manrope sans-serif body and DM Mono utility labels. The original Avel mark is a small four-point star and wordmark.

Compose the page from a landscape hero and product preview, a fictional logo belt, four benefits, a selectable model family, three steps, a company-logo orbit with a fictional testimonial, FAQ, demo signup and footer. The models are Avel Core for everyday tasks, Avel Reason for deeper questions and Avel Spark for creative work. Selection must update the model description and example response. The orbit must continuously rotate counterclockwise, with each logo staying upright; pause it offscreen and under reduced motion. Keep the company names and testimonial explicitly fictional.

Use calm, warm motion: drift and settle. Animate entrances with subtle position and scale, include button and focus feedback, pause loops offscreen, show all content when JavaScript is unavailable and honor reduced motion. Use semantic controls, accessible labels, keyboard support, responsive images and a local-only signup demonstration that sends or stores nothing. Do not use WebGL, Three.js, canvas or 3D. Include metadata, a favicon, accessible contrast, and desktop and mobile layouts.

The hero and closing image sources and exact generation prompts are documented in docs/image-prompts/avel.md. Use the processed local responsive image assets. Do not deploy or commit unless asked.
