# Prompt: rebuild Mio Hazuki — a digital artist's little worlds

Build an isolated, complete Next.js 16 App Router + React + TypeScript + Tailwind v4 landing template for a fictional digital illustrator, Mio Hazuki, at /mio. The purpose is personal commissions. Runtime dependency: lenis, dynamically loaded on fine-pointer desktop only.

## World and concept
Every commission begins with a small wish, so the hero's paper lantern lifts from the artist's seaside studio as the visitor scrolls toward the commission invitation. Use original Japanese animation inspired 2D scenery, everyday magic, quiet intimacy and nostalgic atmosphere. No 3D, model files, stock photography, generated hero images, copied characters, or existing studio branding. All images, including portfolio samples, are original inline SVG. The user explicitly requested coded scenery and waived brand-board approval.

## Identity
Motto: Little worlds, made just for you. Voice: sincere, observant, gentle, specific. Fonts through next/font/google: Kaisei Decol 400/500 display (preload false), Karla body, Inconsolata for price/year data. Distinct variable names. Eight colors only: ink #232C49, paper #F5EFE4, hydrangea #A8B1D1, sea #526D89, apricot #EDAD83, coral #BC6268, plum #654C65, cloud #D8CAD9. Use color-mix and SVG gradients for tints. Readable ink-on-paper and paper-on-ink text. Original lantern-and-wave SVG logo, favicon and next/og share card.

## Composition
The full-bleed, viewport-height hero is a rich twilight harbor with a small tiled-roof studio on the right-hand cliff, illuminated windows, layered mountains, distant waterfront houses, a sailboat, grasses and little flowers. A cloudbank and moon fill the sky. Left-set serif headline: A little wonder, made just for you. Intro: Hi, I'm Mio. I draw quiet adventures, curious souls, and places that feel a little like home. Primary CTA goes to commissions; secondary goes to work. Slim navigation, availability note, pause control and shoreline page transition. Mobile explicitly repositions the SVG below the headline so the studio remains visible.

Portfolio: four independent SVG compositions and a staggered editorial grid, filter buttons, and native detail dialogs with Escape and focus restoration. Works: Where the light stays (coastal home), The cloud keeper (original traveler with a cloud jar and a white fox), One last delivery (bicycle outside a lit night shop), An afternoon, forever (cat beside an open-window artist's desk). Label these as original sample works for a fictional portfolio.

About: illustrated artist at her desk, a personal note about everyday wonder, and a signature. Pricing: A familiar face, character portrait from $120, 1–2 weeks; A little moment, character and setting from $280, 2–3 weeks; A world of your own, full scene from $560, 3–5 weeks. Clearly list scope, revisions, digital delivery, personal use, commercial quote, and 50% deposit/balance. These are sample prices, not real claims. Selecting a scope preselects it in the form.

Four-stage process: daydream, agreed quote and sketch, revisions and color, final delivery. Dark invitation: Your daydream. My paintbrush. Form: name, email, package, idea. Validate name/email, allowlisted package and story length. Show errors with focus on the first invalid input, then loading and a lantern success state. It is a clearly labeled local demo: no network request or storage, no assertion of a sent message. Let the visitor copy/read the prepared brief and start another. Finish with four native FAQ disclosures and a footer: logo, tagline and availability note, Explore and Commissions link columns that jump to real sections, a bottom row with copyright, back to the harbor and a start-a-brief link, then a full-width original SVG scene that fades up out of the paper — a lantern-lit seaside village on a headland, a red-and-white lighthouse, a sailboat, round blossoming trees and grass with coral flowers, with a paper lantern floating above the village. It uses only the eight palette colors, drifts clouds, bobs the lantern and sways the foreground in three ambient groups that pause offscreen and under reduced motion.

## Motion and quality
Organic / analog, verbs sway and unfurl. Masked hero lines, staggered nav/copy/actions, three restrained ambient groups, small 2D layer offsets, one scroll-driven lantern ascent. Lantern progress is clamp(scrollY / heroHeight,0,1), x=-45p, y=-170p, rotation=-4+9p. A page progress hairline. Use shared cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1), cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), cubic-bezier(.77,0,.175,1), spring and 90/180/420/800/1200ms tokens. Reveal vocabulary: rise, mask, wipe, scale and draw. Hover lifts artwork, scales media and translates arrows; press feedback scale .97. No CSS 3D, tilt, autoplay audio or hover-only information. Stop ambient work offscreen and in hidden tabs; provide a scene pause button. Reduced motion disables position movement and Lenis. Without JS all content and art stay visible. Real semantic controls, strong contrast, visible focus, usable 390px touch layout and no horizontal overflow.

Split into brand, art, harbor, sections, works, forms and motion folders. Test pure progress/lantern math and validation, plus browser navigation, filters, dialog, scope preselection, local success, mobile menu, reduced motion and no-JS. Register palette, gallery prompt and screenshot preview, copy bundle, image-processing allowlist, metadata expectations and shared e2e path. Include DESIGN.md and optional replacement-art directions. All delivered art is complete; no media waiting on the user. Do not deploy or commit.
