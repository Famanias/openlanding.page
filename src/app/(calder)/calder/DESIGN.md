# CALDER & ROWE — counsel for consequential decisions

## Concept

Calder & Rowe is an independent law firm for leaders making decisions with no easy precedent. The page treats the firm like a well-reasoned brief: a clear opening position, a method, the matters it handles, the people behind the work, and a direct invitation to bring the hard one.

## Palette and type

- Black `#000000`: text and fine details
- Dark blue `#102B3F`: the firm’s institutional anchor and footer
- White `#FFFFFF`: page surfaces and reversed type
- Cormorant Garamond: refined legal display type
- Source Serif 4: long-form body copy
- Lato: navigation, metadata, forms, and interactive elements

## Motion

- Reveal copy with opacity and translateY when it crosses an IntersectionObserver threshold.
- No decorative parallax or chapter rail: legal content stays stable and easy to scan.
- Hover motion is limited to button lift and rule expansion. Reduced motion removes all transitions.

## Sections

1. Hero: “Practical counsel for difficult decisions.” with a consultation CTA and firm details.
2. Services: concrete practice areas with short descriptions.
3. Our firm: attorney profile, experience facts and direct-attention promise.
4. Proof and FAQs: a client quote plus common questions.
5. Contact: consultation form, phone, email and office address.

## Signature details

- Circular CR mark, direct phone number and consultation CTA.
- A visible practice list, attorney profile, FAQs and contact form.
- The visual language stays in the conventions of a real professional-services site instead of abstract concept-page decoration.
- The whole template is self-contained under `src/app/(calder)/`.
