# Expedition timing estimates

The planner uses fixed, rounded ranges for conventional supported expeditions. They include the approach, acclimatization, rest/weather allowance and return, rather than only days spent climbing above base camp. Pre-acclimatized fast-track programs are excluded. These are indicative planning estimates, not a promised operator schedule.

**Summit push** means the final rotation from base camp through the high camps to the summit and back to base camp. It is included in the expedition total. It does not mean the final summit-day climb from the highest camp, which operators sometimes also call a “summit push” and describe in hours.

Total-duration windows are rounded from the primary operator itineraries below. Summit-rotation ranges are editorial estimates inferred from the standard camp sequences and ascent/return allowance, rather than quoted operator commitments. Everest's 50–60 day total and 4–6 day summit rotation follow the user's supplied example. References checked on 24 October 2026; update `expedition-timings.ts` when reviewing these estimates.
