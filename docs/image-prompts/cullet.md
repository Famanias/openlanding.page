# Cullet: image prompts

Cullet ships without photography: the hero and the sampler are real-time WebGL, and the material swatches are drawn in CSS. These prompts are **optional** studio renders that replace the six drawn swatches in the library (`Picture` picks them up automatically).

Save files to `assets/incoming/cullet/` with the exact filenames below, then run `npm run images -- cullet`.

## Style preamble (paste into every prompt)

> High-key product photography of a single cut glass sample on a seamless pale blue-grey studio sweep (#F4F5F8 to #DCE8FF), soft overhead softbox plus a cool fill from below, crisp specular highlights, subtle caustics on the floor, shallow depth of field, 100mm macro lens, clinical and quiet, generous negative space. No text, no logos, no hands, no props, no colour other than what the glass itself refracts.

## Prompts

**material-crown-silica.png**
[preamble] A thick slab of clear crown glass, cut as an irregular five-sided shard with bevelled, polished edges. Almost colourless, very low dispersion, clean edge refraction of the background gradient, a faint green tint only inside the thickest edge.

**material-dense-flint.png**
[preamble] A heavy flint-glass prism shard with sharp bevels, throwing vivid rainbow caustics across the floor, strong dispersion fringes at every edge, a deep cool tint in the body.

**material-frosted-quartz.png**
[preamble] A shard of frosted quartz: satin-etched faces under a polished clearcoat rim, milky white body, a pearlescent sky-cyan-and-rose thin-film sheen across the top face.

**material-borosilicate.png**
[preamble] A thin, tough sheet of laboratory borosilicate glass cut into a shard with fire-polished edges, almost no colour fringing, a pale mint cast where the edges are thickest.

**material-sapphire.png**
[preamble] A small, perfectly polished synthetic sapphire crystal window, cut as a five-sided polygon, extremely clear with very bright, hard specular edges and almost no rainbow.

**material-opal-film.png**
[preamble] A soft, translucent shard under a thin iridescent film that shifts from cobalt to mint to pearl across its face, diffused body, gentle frosted roughness.

No video is used.
