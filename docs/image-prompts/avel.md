Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: atmospheric wide hero background for a premium AI model company's website. Create an original editorial landscape photograph of a sunlit Mediterranean coastline: a monumental pale sandstone cliff occupies the far right edge, delicate wind-shaped dark pine on its upper rim, distant limestone headlands and a tranquil muted deep pine green sea along the lower third. The top two thirds is an open, luminous hazy sky in soft pastel clay-brown and warm sand, with generous uncluttered space across center and left for dark website typography. Photographic, quietly cinematic, natural light, subtle atmospheric grain and texture, refined contemporary architecture magazine art direction. Wide landscape composition. No people, no interface, no graphics, no letters, no logos, no watermarks. Avoid neon colors, oversaturation, fantasy, 3D renders.

## closing.png — wide landscape, closing and footer background

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide background photograph for the closing call-to-action of a premium AI landing page, visually related to a hero photograph of sunlit Mediterranean cliffs, a dark pine green sea, and a warm sandy sky. Create a calm, original companion photograph of a Mediterranean coastal inlet at golden hour: soft layered pale limestone headlands recede into the distance, a few dark pines frame the extreme edges, and still deep muted pine water sits low in the composition. Most of the image should be an open luminous pastel clay and sand colored sky, especially through the center, providing ample uninterrupted light negative space for dark green text. Wide panoramic editorial composition with an atmospheric sense of depth, natural light, warm restrained earth tones, subtle photographic grain, and a sophisticated architecture-magazine quality. No people, buildings, typography, logos, interface elements, watermarks, 3D rendering, neon, or fantasy.
