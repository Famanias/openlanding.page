# AUBADE — image prompts

Seven photographs, all **inside the carriage**. The outside world (the window landscapes) is drawn as flat SVG colour fields, so never generate a landscape photo.

Save raw files to `assets/incoming/aubade/` under the exact filenames below (PNG or JPG, ≥ 2000 px on the long side), then run `npm run images -- aubade`.

## Style preamble (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Medium-format film photograph, Kodak Portra 400 pushed one stop, the interior of a 1930s-style luxury sleeper train carriage at night, warm tungsten lamplight against deep shadow, colour held strictly to claret-red velvet, deep fir green, linen white, night indigo and soft apricot, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, still and quiet, editorial interior photography. No people's faces (people only from behind or as hands), no text, no logos, no signage, no brand marks.

## Prompts

### berth-couchette.jpg (4:3)
[Style preamble] A single private couchette berth in a narrow sleeper compartment: a made-up bed 190 cm long with crisp linen-white sheets and a folded indigo wool blanket, a small brass reading lamp glowing warm, a compact wood-veneer washbasin cabinet, claret velvet on the wall panel, and the window blind half-lowered with only darkness outside. Shot from the doorway at eye level, 35 mm equivalent, intimate and orderly.

### berth-cabine.jpg (4:3)
[Style preamble] A two-berth sleeper cabin with an upper and lower bunk made up in linen-white percale, a short brass ladder, a fold-down table holding two small glasses and a carafe of water, fir-green upholstered window seat, claret velvet curtains tied back, the window showing deep indigo night. Lamplight from two wall sconces. Wide 28 mm view from the corridor door, symmetrical composition.

### berth-grandlit.jpg (4:3)
[Style preamble] The finest cabin on the train: a double bed 140 cm wide, turned down, with linen-white percale and a claret velvet headboard, a brass reading lamp lit on each side, a folded apricot-coloured cashmere throw at the foot, a door ajar to a small private shower clad in dark green tile, the window blind raised to deep indigo darkness. Shot from the foot of the bed, 35 mm, calm and generous.

### dining-car.jpg (3:2)
[Style preamble] The dining car at the first dinner seating: a long narrow carriage of fir-green leather banquettes and tables laid with white cloths, small brass table lamps with apricot shades glowing down the length of the car in perspective, polished glassware, a single guest seen from behind raising a glass, windows on the left reflecting the lamps against black night. Low 35 mm angle down the aisle, deep perspective, shallow depth of field.

### night-turndown.jpg (4:5)
[Style preamble] Close detail of a sleeper bed at turndown: a gloved attendant's hands folding back a linen-white sheet over an indigo blanket, a small square of dark chocolate wrapped in claret paper on the pillow, a brass lamp glowing at the edge of frame. Overhead three-quarter view, 50 mm, raking warm light, tactile textures of percale and wool.

### night-quiet.jpg (4:5)
[Style preamble] The sleeper corridor at midnight: a long narrow passage with a run of closed wood-veneer cabin doors, claret carpet runner, small numbered brass door plates catching dim night lighting, windows on one side showing pure indigo darkness, nobody present. Centered one-point perspective, 35 mm, hushed and almost dark, a single warm sconce at the far end.

### night-bell.jpg (4:5)
[Style preamble] Early morning in a sleeper cabin: a breakfast tray on the folded bed with a white porcelain coffee cup, a croissant and a small brass hand bell, soft apricot dawn light pouring through a half-raised window blind and laying warm stripes across linen sheets, a hint of pale blue water beyond the glass, out of focus. 50 mm, gentle backlight, the mood of waking somewhere else.
