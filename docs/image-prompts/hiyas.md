# HIYAS image and video prompts

The user will generate the media from these prompts. Put raw stills in `assets/incoming/hiyas/` and raw clips in `assets/incoming/hiyas/clips/` using the exact filenames below. The template keeps labelled placeholders until those files arrive.

## Style preamble

Documentary high-altitude expedition photography, shot before sunrise or in hard alpine side light, black rock, white ice, deep dark blue shadows, visible film grain, restrained contrast, real equipment and weather texture, calm observation instead of heroic posing. No logos, no flags, no text, no watermarks, no fantasy neon, no visible faces, no unsafe stunts, no staged summit celebration. Leave clean dark areas for white route data overlays.
