# MESA MARKET image manifest

All twelve images are sourced from the authorized Mesa Market design kit under `assets/incoming/mesamarket`; no external stock photo CDNs are used. Files are processed locally into WebP and AVIF variants with `npm run images -- mesamarket`.

| --- | --- | --- |

| `02-carrot-alpha` | 1024 × 1024 | Nantes bunched carrots with feathery green tops, framed sticker cutout disc. |
| `03-lemon-alpha` | 1024 × 1024 | Bright Meyer orchard lemons, framed sticker cutout disc. |
| `04-kale-alpha` | 1024 × 1024 | Deep blue-green Lacinato cavolo nero kale bunch, framed sticker cutout disc. |
| `05-pepper-alpha` | 1024 × 1024 | Sweet Jimmy Nardello frying peppers, framed sticker cutout disc. |

All pricing, farm locations, recipe descriptions, and receipt line items are rendered as accessible HTML and styled with typography tokens. The delivery map and producer route are rendered via responsive inline SVG.
