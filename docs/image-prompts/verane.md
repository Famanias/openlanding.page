# Image and video prompts: MAISON VÉRANE

## Style preamble

Include this preamble in every prompt to keep the atmosphere unified:

> Medium format still-life photograph (Hasselblad H6D, 80mm f/2.8 lens). Soft, diffused northern daylight falling from a high studio window onto honed French limestone and unbleached Belgian linen. Minimalist quiet luxury, restrained composition with ample negative space, sharp tactile texture, shallow depth of field with graceful falloff. Desaturated natural earth and mineral tones with a very subtle cool violet/orris nuance in the deepest shadows. Palette of charcoal-oud (#2A1E22), limestone chalk (#E3DFD8), pale orris purple (#655A7E), muted ash (#5F5753), raw linen (#CFC9C0), and faint antique brass (#B59A6A). No people, no faces, no floating ingredients, no digital glow, no neon, no watermark, no text, no synthetic CGI gloss.
