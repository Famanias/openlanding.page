# TOURNE image prompts

No image or video API keys were detected, so the user generates these on any platform, drops the raw files in `assets/incoming/tourne/` using the filenames below, then runs `npm run images -- tourne`.

Shared direction: warm natural window light, matte stoneware, terracotta, bone and celadon tones, soot-black accents, real hands and real clay dust, 35mm editorial photography, no text, no logos, no watermarks.

| Key | Aspect | Used in |

### `potter-hana`

A woman in her late thirties in a linen apron, hands wet with slip, pulling the wall of a tall pale vase on an electric wheel. Converted boatyard studio, tall windows, grey Puget Sound light, wooden shelves of unglazed pots behind her. Shallow depth of field, matte earthy palette of terracotta, bone and celadon. Portrait orientation 4:5. No text.

### `potter-teo`

A tall man in his forties crouching beside an open gas kiln, holding a freshly fired celadon bowl up to the light, soot on his forearms, firing logbook open on a stool. Warm orange glow from the kiln mouth against cool window light. Portrait orientation 4:5. No text.
