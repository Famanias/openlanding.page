# ALPINE REEL — image prompts

Fourteen wide mountain photographs, one per eight-thousander. Each fills a full-viewport slide, so every image is a **5:2 panorama** with the summit centred.

Save raw files to `assets/incoming/alpine-reel/` under the exact filenames below (PNG or JPG, **3200 × 1280 px**, 5:2), then run `npm run images -- alpine-reel`. If your generator can't do 5:2, make it 16:9 and crop top and bottom only, never the sides.

## Style preamble (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Cinematic high-altitude documentary photograph, ultra-wide 5:2 panorama, large-format camera with a long lens, cold overcast blue-hour light, restrained blue-grey monochrome tonality from deep shadow to clean pale snow, sharp rock strata and fine snow texture, layered atmospheric haze, a thin wind plume off the summit. The summit apex sits at 50% across and 30% down the frame; the upper 30% is quiet sky with no detail; the lower-left third is dark, low-detail foreground. No people, no tents, no flags, no climbing gear, no buildings, no text, no logos, no warm sunset tones.

## Prompts

### mount-everest.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] Mount Everest seen from the southwest: a dark pyramid of banded limestone and snow rising behind the Nuptse–Lhotse wall, a long jet-stream plume of snow streaming east from the summit, the shadowed Khumbu glacier in the lower foreground.

### k2-godwin-austen.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] K2 seen from the Baltoro glacier: a steep, near-symmetrical pyramid of dark granite with hanging ice on every face, the Abruzzi ridge in profile on the left flank, a banner of storm cloud on the summit, moraine and glacial ice in the dark foreground.

### kangchenjunga.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] Kangchenjunga, a vast massif of five snow summits seen from the east in monsoon season, fluted white faces, layers of monsoon haze between dark forested ridges in the foreground, the highest summit slightly forward of the others.

### lhotse.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] The south face of Lhotse: a colossal vertical wall of blue-grey ice and rock rising above the Nuptse ridge, the summit a narrow snow crown at the top of the wall, a deep shadowed valley below.

### makalu.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] Makalu, an isolated four-sided pyramid of dark granite with knife-edge snow arêtes running down each corner, a sharp summit against a pale sky, a dark glacial basin in the foreground.

### cho-oyu.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] Cho Oyu at twilight: a broad, gently angled mountain with long flanks of wind-packed snow and a flat summit plateau, the Ngozumpa glacier as a dark band in the foreground, calm cold blue-hour light.

### dhaulagiri-i.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] Dhaulagiri I rising abruptly above the Kali Gandaki gorge: an enormous white massif with fluted ice faces and a summit pyramid, the deep dark river gorge filling the lower foreground.

### manaslu.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] Manaslu, a long sweeping ridge with a double-horned rock and snow summit, hanging valley glaciers on its flanks, seen across a dark glacial moraine, soft cloud drifting below the ridge.

### nanga-parbat.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] The Rupal Face of Nanga Parbat: an immense 4,600-metre wall of rock, ice and hanging glaciers rising from a dark pine-and-meadow valley floor, the summit pyramid at the top of the wall.

### annapurna-i.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] Annapurna I from inside its sanctuary: an amphitheatre of steep snow ridges and hanging seracs around a high summit, avalanche chutes streaking the faces, a small dark rocky moraine in the foreground.

### gasherbrum-i.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] Gasherbrum I (Hidden Peak) at the head of the Baltoro glacier: a tapering triangular summit of dark rock and snow, partly hidden behind nearer rocky ridges, a wide remote valley of glacial ice below.

### broad-peak.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] Broad Peak: a long, broad summit crest of snow running horizontally across the frame with a gentle shoulder at one end, seen across the Godwin-Austen glacier, rock-strewn ice in the foreground.

### gasherbrum-ii.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] Gasherbrum II: a delicate shark-fin ice pyramid with a sharp summit arête, rising above a sea of low cloud and the upper Baltoro glacier, cold clean air.

### shishapangma.jpg (5:2)
[Style preamble] Shishapangma seen from the Tibetan plateau: a single broad dome-like snow summit with a fluted southwest face of snow and dark granite, a wide high grassy plain and a dark glacial stream in the foreground, vast open sky.
