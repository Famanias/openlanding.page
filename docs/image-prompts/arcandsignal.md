## signal-horizon.png

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide hero artwork for an editorial digital marketing agency landing page. Create a premium, atmospheric abstract image inspired by luminous signals and momentum: long, fine violet, lilac and icy-blue light trails arc gracefully across a very dark midnight-indigo field, converging in a radiant soft focal glow near the lower center. Include subtle transparent photographic glass reflections and restrained film texture, with the sweeping, dramatic curved horizon of a high-end campaign image. Sophisticated and cinematic, with plenty of clean dark negative space around the upper half; wide landscape composition. No lettering, no logos, no words, no numbers, no interface or charts, no people, no watermark. This is a flat raster artwork for a website, not an interactive 3D scene.
