# Snoot: image prompts

Twelve photographs for the Snoot vet clinic template. Everything else on the page (Frank, the pets, the ball, the stamps) is drawn in code. Until a file exists, the page shows a labelled placeholder with the key below.

**Save raw images to** `assets/incoming/snoot/` using the exact filename (any of `.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`), then run `npm run images -- snoot`.

## Style preamble (paste into every prompt)

> Bright, warm, candid editorial photograph of a friendly neighbourhood veterinary clinic. Natural daylight from big windows, soft fill, gentle film-like grain, shot on a 35mm lens at f/2.8 with shallow depth of field. Clean, modern interior with biscuit-cream walls, pops of cobalt blue, warm yolk yellow and soft pink in props and scrubs. Joyful, calm, reassuring mood; pets look relaxed and curious, never distressed. No text, no logos, no signage lettering, no watermarks. Realistic proportions, natural fur detail.

## Prompts

### `team-adaeze` (4:5)
[Style preamble] Portrait of a warm Black woman veterinarian in her late forties with short natural hair, navy scrubs and a cobalt-blue stethoscope, kneeling on a pale wooden clinic floor while a calm grey retired greyhound leans its head into her shoulder. She is smiling at the dog, not the camera. Cream wall behind, soft window light from the left.

### `team-ruth` (4:5)
[Style preamble] Portrait of a Scandinavian woman surgeon in her thirties with a blonde braid and navy surgical scrubs and cap, holding a relaxed three-legged brown tabby cat against her shoulder in a bright prep room. The cat looks toward the camera; she laughs softly. Out-of-focus yolk-yellow towels on a shelf behind.

### `team-kenji` (4:5)
[Style preamble] Portrait of a Japanese man veterinarian in his forties with round glasses and a pink scrub top, smiling as a bearded dragon perches calmly on his forearm. Warm basking-lamp glow from above right, cream wall, a small potted plant out of focus.

### `team-sol` (4:5)
[Style preamble] Portrait of a Latina veterinary nurse in her late twenties with curly dark hair tied up, mint-green scrubs, cradling a fluffy lop-eared rabbit and laughing. Bright consult room, a cobalt-blue weighing scale out of focus behind.

### `visit-door` (4:3)
[Style preamble] Exterior of a small corner clinic on a leafy city street: two side-by-side painted front doors, one cobalt blue and one soft pink, each with a simple round porthole window, a bench and a water bowl between them. Morning sun, a few fallen leaves. No lettering anywhere.

### `visit-treats` (4:3)
[Style preamble] Inside a sunny consult room, a veterinary nurse crouches and offers a small treat from her open palm to a slightly nervous beagle sitting on a blue padded floor mat. The beagle sniffs the hand. Owner's legs and hand on the lead visible at the edge of frame.

### `visit-floor` (4:3)
[Style preamble] A vet sitting cross-legged on a padded cobalt-blue floor mat listening to the chest of a large golden retriever with a stethoscope; the dog is lying down relaxed with its tongue out. Low camera angle at floor level, big window light.

### `visit-walkout` (4:3)
[Style preamble] An owner seen from behind, mid-stride, walking out of a bright clinic doorway onto a sunny pavement with a happy wagging corgi on a yolk-yellow lead. Motion in the tail, warm afternoon light, street trees.

### `patient-dog` (1:1)
[Style preamble] A scruffy wire-haired terrier sitting upright on a cobalt-blue exam mat, head tilted, looking up at the camera with bright eyes. Cream wall behind, soft top light. Square composition, dog centred.

### `patient-cat` (1:1)
[Style preamble] A ginger cat peering out of an open grey carrier placed on a cream towel, whiskers forward, curious and calm. Soft pink blanket edge in the foreground. Square composition.

### `patient-rabbit` (1:1)
[Style preamble] A grey-and-white mini lop rabbit nibbling a sprig of parsley on a soft cream blanket, ears down, side view. Square composition, warm window light.

### `patient-tortoise` (1:1)
[Style preamble] A small Hermann's tortoise stepping across a sunlit pale exam table toward a single strawberry, low angle, shallow depth of field. Square composition.
