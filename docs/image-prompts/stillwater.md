# Stillwater image direction

Six images were generated with the built-in image tool and embedded as optimized WebP data in the standalone HTML. They are fictional architectural scenes with no real property claims.

- **Hero:** A monumental warm-limestone coastal villa on a rugged cliff at blue hour, warm glass interiors, a still infinity pool and the sea; villa on the right, naturally dark sea and foliage on the left.
- **Mobile hero:** A portrait adaptation of the same villa, pulled back to keep the building and pool visible within a narrow frame.
- **Terrace:** A secluded limestone terrace, infinity pool, olive foliage and distant coastline in soft morning light.
- **Lounge:** A restrained limestone and linen living room with floor-to-ceiling glass and a coastal outlook.
- **Bedroom:** Natural linen, warm stone and an open view to the sea in late afternoon light.
- **Coast:** A panoramic rocky coastline and calm sea at dusk, with dark pines and a peach horizon.

All images are high-end hospitality editorial photographs without people, signage, logos or text.
