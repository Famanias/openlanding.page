# AMAOTO media direction

All shipped visuals are procedural code. Do not generate or download images, video, sound or environment maps for this template.

The four local wagasa photos in `assets/incoming/amaoto/ref/` are reconstruction references only. Follow their pleated closed silhouette, shallow bowed canopy, under-canopy lattice, blossom branches and turned wood details. The procedural model is an interpretation rather than a calibrated photographic replica.

Backgrounds use layered inline SVG and CSS: low-contrast washi, sumi ridges and mist with a gold sun; drafting lines and asanoha; an indigo moon with ink clouds and shared rain; sakura petals and dye wash; bamboo silhouettes; dusk and puddles. Use only the registered seven-colour palette. Repeated details must belong to the mechanism and move with it. The fallback and gallery preview are authored SVG.

Rain is instanced WebGL streaks, rim drips and puddle rings. Audio is gesture-created Web Audio filtered noise and sparse drips, off by default. No external media service is involved.
