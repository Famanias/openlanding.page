# Image prompts — SUNDIAL Coffee Roasters

You generate these on any platform you like. Drop the finished files into `assets/incoming/sundial/` under the exact filenames, then run `npm run images -- sundial`.

## Style preamble (paste into every prompt)

Modern specialty-coffee retail photography, bright and appetizing: warm morning daylight from a large window, cream and kraft surfaces, matte-white and kraft coffee bags with pill-shaped labels in tangerine orange and deep roast green, honest saturated colour, soft natural shadows, shallow but not dreamy depth of field (50mm, f/2.8). Sunny, bold, friendly — never moody-luxury, never dark-and-smoky. No text, no logos, no brand marks, no people's faces, no watermarks.

### 1. `lot-kraft-bag.png`

{preamble}

A single 340 g kraft coffee bag standing on a pale oat-cream counter, sealed with a tin tie, a blank rounded-rectangle label in tangerine orange, one roasted coffee bean resting beside it, a few loose beans scattered. Straight-on product framing, generous negative space above. The bag is unbranded and completely free of text.

### 2. `counter-scene.png`

{preamble}

A modern café roastery counter seen slightly from above: a cream-painted bar, a row of kraft bags on an oak shelf, a pill-shaped tangerine orange sticker sheet, a brass scoop, a small steel roasting drum in soft background blur. No people, no faces. Editorial retail warmth, generous empty space on the left for a headline.

### 3. `pour-over.png`

{preamble}

Macro of a V60 pour-over in progress: a thin gooseneck kettle stream hitting a blooming coffee bed, steam catching the morning light, crema-coloured ceramic dripper on a matte-black scale, deep roast green accent on the scale's display bezel (no readable text). Crisp droplets, appetizing, bright.

### 4. `hero-bag.png`

{preamble}

Three kraft coffee bags in a row on a crema-white surface, shot from a low three-quarter angle, each with a blank pill-shaped label in a different brand colour (tangerine, roast green, espresso brown). Sunlit, clean, retail-catalogue crispness, plenty of headroom.
