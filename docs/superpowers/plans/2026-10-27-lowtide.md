# LOWTIDE Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add `/lowtide`, a gallery template that is one full-screen three.js night on a moonlit tidal flat, where scrolling is the clock and five low tides tell a sleep ring's story.

**Architecture:** A server-rendered "tide table" holds every word of content (it is the no-JS / reduced-motion page and, in experience mode, the visually hidden accessible mirror). A client `Experience` leaf lazy-loads a WebGL engine onto a fixed canvas, drives it from Lenis + GSAP ScrollTrigger over an invisible scroll track, and feeds it one `FrameInput` per rAF (progress, roughness, calm, lantern). All timing maths (clock, tide, chapter weights, roughness, calm, night plan, validation) are pure, tested modules.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16.4 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, three 0.186 (`WebGLRenderer`, `examples/jsm` Water/Sky/GPUComputationRenderer/EffectComposer/RoomEnvironment), troika-three-text (new), tone (new), gsap 3.15 + ScrollTrigger, lenis 1.3, Vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-lowtide-design.md` — read it before any task; it carries the anti-goals table, the story table and the product facts.

## Review Focus

- **Clock crossing midnight / wake before bed:** `planNight("23:30","07:00")` must treat wake as next day; equal times and > 16 h are errors, not 0 or negative cycles. (Task 3 tests.)
- **Scroll position out of range or restored mid-night on reload:** progress < 0 or > 1 (overscroll, restored scroll) must clamp everywhere; a reload mid-page must render that moment, not chapter 0. (Task 2 clamp tests; Task 5 e2e reload test.)
- **WebGL unavailable or context lost:** the page must fall back to the visible tide table, not a black screen. (Task 5: `canMountExperience` test + e2e with WebGL disabled via `--disable-gpu --disable-software-rasterizer` is unreliable, so unit-test the gate and handle `webglcontextlost` by switching mode.)
- **Keyboard users never touching the mouse:** lantern rests centre with a wide radius, the dial steps chapters, Space/arrows don't fire while focus is in a form field. (Task 5 e2e: typing a space in the email field doesn't move the night.)
- **Fast flick to the end then immediate back:** snap must not fight the user; the snap tween is cancelled by any new wheel/touch input. (Task 5 e2e: wheel during snap leaves progress where the user put it.)
